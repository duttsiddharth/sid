AI Resilience by Design

What happens when your AI fails?

AI resilience means designing operations that keep running when AI is unavailable, wrong, replaced or more autonomous than you intended. AI Resilience by Design is how SD Advisory builds that in from the start.

Always onBusiness processesstay stable in every mode

AI ON

AI-assisted operations

  • AI modelsActive · replaceable
  • AutomationAI-assisted triage, correlation, drafting
  • Human governanceGoverned guardrails

Full capability: AI-assisted triage, correlation and drafting, inside governed guardrails.

AI DEGRADED

Human-governed fallback

  • AI modelsFallback engaged
  • AutomationLower-risk only
  • Human governanceApproves higher-risk actions

Fallbacks engage. Lower-risk automation continues; higher-risk actions need human approval.

AI OFF

Deterministic operations

  • AI modelsOff
  • AutomationDeterministic runbooks
  • Human governanceDefined non-AI path

Deterministic runbooks take over. Every business-critical capability keeps a defined non-AI path.

Always onGovernance · Observability · Auditabilitymodel abstraction keeps providers replaceable

The AI can change. The business doesn’t stop.

Prototype accelerator

SmartDesk

42%Ticket auto-resolution
Read case study →
Problem
Tier-1 analysts classify, prioritise and route ServiceNow tickets by hand — slow and inconsistent.
Approach
Manual triage → AI classification → routing → response, with P1/P0 always escalated to a human.
Technology
Claude API, n8n, ServiceNow, Slack.
Outcome
About 42% auto-resolution and ~2.5 hours a day of triage saved in the prototype.
Prototype accelerator

AI Incident Co-Pilot

20%MTTR reduction
Read case study →
Problem
During major incidents, correlating signals and drafting stakeholder updates compete for the responder’s attention.
Approach
Observability → AI analysis → remediation guidance → stakeholder communication, every output reviewed by a human.
Technology
LLM-based analysis and retrieval; designed for Dynatrace and Splunk signals.
Outcome
20% MTTR reduction in the prototype.
Delivery engagement

SLA Transformation

Read case study →
Problem
96% SLA adherence, with inconsistent escalation handling and loosely governed vendor performance.
Approach
Escalation matrix → AI-driven monitoring → vendor governance → executive reporting.
Technology
ITIL 4, Dynatrace, Splunk, AI-driven workflow automation.
Outcome
SLA adherence from 96% to 99.8%.
