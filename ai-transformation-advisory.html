// The enterprise problem

GenAI experiments are easy to start and hard to scale

Most technology leaders now have several AI experiments running: a chatbot here, a copilot there. The harder questions sit above the tooling. Which use cases are worth industrialising? Who owns the risk? What is the operating model once a pilot works? What happens to operations on the day a model, a provider or a price changes?

AI transformation advisory answers those questions before they become incidents — in the order a CIO needs them answered.

// What the advisory covers

Seven decisions, made deliberately

AI strategy

Where AI fits in your technology and operations strategy, and where it deliberately does not.

AI readiness

An honest read of process maturity, data, tooling and governance. Start with the free AI Readiness Assessment.

AI roadmap

A sequenced plan of pilots and platform decisions, tied to operational outcomes rather than technology milestones.

AI operating model

Roles, decision rights, funding and support model for AI-enabled operations after the pilot phase.

AI governance

Risk tiers, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, auditability and policy that engineers can actually follow.

Use-case prioritisation

High-value, low-risk opportunities first — scored on value, feasibility, risk and operational fit.

Enterprise AI architecture

Model abstraction, retrieval, agents and integration patterns designed for provider independence. See AI Resilience by Design.

// How we work

We don’t sell AI first. We identify the right problem first.

Every engagement follows the SD Advisory AI Transformation Method: Discover → Assess → Prioritise → Pilot → Prove → Scale. You decide at each step whether to continue.

  • Discover and assess — operational context, AI readiness, process maturity and governance.
  • Prioritise and pilot — one focused, human-in-the-loop use case with agreed before/after metrics.
  • Prove and scale — measure ROI, impact and risk; then industrialise architecture, governance and automation.

// Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you recommend specific AI vendors or platforms?

SD Advisory is technology-agnostic. Recommendations follow your operating context, governance requirements and existing platforms such as ServiceNow, not a vendor relationship.

Where should we start?

With the three-minute AI Readiness Assessment. It gives you a readiness score, the priority gaps and recommended actions, and a clear basis for a 30-minute consultation.

Is AI safe to put in front of production operations?

It can be, if the design assumes AI will sometimes be unavailable or wrong. That is the premise of AI Resilience by Design: fallbacks, human approval for higher-risk actions and a defined non-AI path for critical capabilities.
