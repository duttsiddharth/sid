// How we label evidence

Honest terminology

  • Production deployment — live in a client environment.
  • Pilot — a scoped trial with a client, measured against an agreed baseline.
  • Prototype accelerator — a working build or live demo used to demonstrate the approach. Its figures are benchmarks of that build, not client results.
  • Delivery engagement — enterprise service-delivery work; the client is not named.
  • Reference architecture — a design model, not a deployment. Figures inside it are illustrative.

Every case study follows the same structure: business problem, challenge, approach, architecture, outcome, technology and lessons.
